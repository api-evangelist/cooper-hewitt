The jazz greats like Billie Holiday and Frank Sinatra sang it best: autumn in New York really does seem so inviting. And as the breeze cools and the leaves begin to turn, it felt like the appropriate time to round up a selection of objects from Cooper Hewitt’s collection that represent the magic of the city this time of year.
