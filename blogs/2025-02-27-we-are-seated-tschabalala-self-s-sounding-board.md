“A chair always implies a figure, even if no figure is occupying it.” —Tschabalala Self Artist Tschabalala Self received a commission from the Performa 2021 Biennial and wrote, directed, and designed her first play, Sounding Board . The play imagines an argument between two people in a relationship—characters A and B—navigating an existential quest within the context of the stage.
