Broadway! Opera! Shakespeare! Hollywood! Drama! Across the performing arts, costume design is integral to the shaping and perception of a character. Costumes can help transport performers and audiences to past times or imaginary worlds, or can ground a theatrical text in the present through contemporary styling. Women costume designers have made some of the most significant contributions to the craft, producing vast bodies of work and iconic sartorial images that become synonymous with character
